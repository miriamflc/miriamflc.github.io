About me

Since 2014, I have wanted to design products that make a real impact on people’s lives. With a background in industrial and service design, I have shaped my career around what interests me most: using design as a tool to build common languages, and exploring the boundaries between art and technology.

After six years at digital agencies, and six more as a product designer for tech companies and startups, I am still passionate about what I do. If you would like to collaborate — or just want to say hi — feel free to get in touch.
