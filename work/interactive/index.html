Akyute
Since 2019 · Barcelona based collective

Akyute is the Barcelona based collective I co-founded in 2019 with Natalia Gima and Mago Hart. In our practice we use technology as a means to reconnect the human experience with the natural world, integrating nature into the digital age, and unifying interaction design, audiovisual arts and generative sound. The interactive experiences below are part of that work.

Plant biofeedback
Ongoing · Biodata sonification project

An ongoing investigation on plants intelligence that started with Akyute's first installation at Mutek ES 2019.

WVN
2019 · Audiovisual installation

A large-format reactive audiovisual piece designed for BAU, Design College of Barcelona.

AURAL
2019 · Interactive installation

An immersive experience that lets participants hack their electromagnetic field through physical interaction with a light sculpture.
