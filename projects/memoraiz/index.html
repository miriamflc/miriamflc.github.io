MemorAIz(2024-2025)

RoleFounding Design Lead (freelance)
ClientMemorAIz
Year2024-2025
ScopeEd-tech products, AI
Project overview
MemorAIz offers adaptive and personalized learning experiences

MemorAIz is an AI startup: for B2B clients, it designs AI-powered learning experiences to plug into their own products; for B2C, it builds ad hoc learning experiences of its own — like the ed-tech product this case study is about.

It transforms the content people study from, such as video, handwriting, audio, complex documents and books, into mind maps, summaries, quizzes, audio summaries, audiobooks, key points and flashcards.

Co-design is part of how the team works with its B2B clients: for H-FARM College, for example, we ran a workshop to co-design a study tool directly with university students, testing and validating early concepts for an in-house AI tutor before building it out.

I lead its experience design and the design team, from the product strategy to the features of its core experiences.

Workshop
AI Learn: co-designing the future of learning
Year2025
FormatDesign thinking workshop
Duration1 day
ParticipantsUniversity students

Students from different university degree programs at H-FARM College took part. The goal: validate early concepts for an AI tutor we were developing in-house, by exploring them directly with students.

Using design thinking methods, we guided the group through key questions around adopting AI tools, how they fit into everyday study processes, and into ideating new, creative approaches to learning.

Many insights and reflections came out of it — a reminder of how important it is to involve students directly in shaping how they engage with the AI shift already reaching education, and useful validation for the AI tutor concepts we were exploring.
